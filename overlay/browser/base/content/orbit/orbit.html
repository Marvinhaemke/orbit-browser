▣ Frames

Press F and draw a frame. Drag cards inside. Rename its heading, resize its corner, or move the heading to move the entire group. Open all its tabs with one click.

◉ Peek & compare

Card actions render real websites through Firefox. Peek keeps your canvas visible. Compare opens the card beside your active page.

⤳ Connections & notes

Press C, then choose two cards or notes. Double-click a line to remove it. Press N and click to place a note, then type into it.

✧ Gestures & keyboard

Right-click for the radial menu. Hold Space and drag to pan. Ctrl + scroll zooms. Arrow keys move a selected card. Delete removes it from the canvas. Ctrl + Z undoes a board change.

Canvas removal keeps the browser tab open. Open tabs stay in Firefox’s native tab strip. Workspaces are saved in this browser profile; private windows keep them only for the session.
