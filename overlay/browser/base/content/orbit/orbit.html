    Follow your curiosity.

    ▣ Frames

    Press F and draw a frame. Drag cards inside. Rename its heading, resize its corner, or move the heading to move the entire group. Open all its tabs with one click.

    ◉ Peek & compare

    Preview a live website while your canvas stays visible. Compare opens the card beside your active page.

    ⤳ Connections & notes

    Press C, then choose two cards or notes. Double-click a line to remove it. Press N and click to place a note, then type into it.

    ✧ Gestures & keyboard

    Right-click for a radial menu that fits what you clicked, then press a letter or click an action. Hold Space and drag to pan. Ctrl + scroll zooms. Arrow keys move a selected card. Delete removes it from the canvas. Ctrl + Z undoes a board change.

    Canvas removal keeps the browser tab open. Open tabs stay in Orbit’s tab strip. Workspaces are saved in this browser profile; private windows keep them only for the session.
